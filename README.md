# FamilyHub

*Working name.* [Русская версия](README.ru.md)

A private space for your family, on your own server. Shared tasks and chores, shopping lists, a family calendar
with relatives' birthdays, a map showing where everyone is, and a family budget — all connected by tags.

> **Status: early planning.** Nothing to install yet. Follow [docs/STATUS.md](docs/STATUS.md) for progress.

## Planned features

- **Tasks** — one-off and recurring chores, assigned and rotated between family members.
- **Shopping lists** — shared, and usable in the store without network.
- **Calendar** — events and birthdays, with who each event concerns, and reminders.
- **Map** — where family members are, places like "Home" and "School" with arrival alerts; GPS trackers and kids' watches later.
- **Budget** — split income into categories by your own rules, track overspending, loans between categories, and debts.
- **Tags** — tag "Car" and see its tasks, events and spending in one place.

## Principles

- **Self-hosted.** Your data stays on your server. No cloud, no telemetry, no ads.
- **Free forever.** Open source under AGPL-3.0, no paid tiers.
- **No Google required.** Works on any Android phone, with or without Google services.
- **Works offline.** Changes sync when you are back online.

## Platforms

Server: Docker Compose on any Linux VPS or home server. App: Android first; web, iOS and desktop later.
