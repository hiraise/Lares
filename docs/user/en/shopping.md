# Shopping lists

Shared family lists that work in a store, even with no network. Items sort themselves by store department, bought
items move to the bottom, and whatever the family bought before comes back as one-tap suggestions.

## Where the lists are

- **Shopping** (the third tab) shows all lists of the family: how much is bought, what is left and who is shopping
  now. Tap a list to open it.
- **Today** (the first tab) shows at the top a card of the list that changed last and still has something to buy:
  "6 of 9", "Left: Milk, Bread, Apples", who is shopping now. **Open** opens that list. If other lists also have
  something to buy, "2 more lists" appears under the card. When there is nothing to buy, there is no card.
- The **tag page** (More › Tags) has a "Shopping" section: items to buy with that tag and lists with that tag.

## Create a list

On the Shopping tab tap **List** and type a name, for example "Groceries", "Pharmacy", "Household". The list menu
(⋮ at the top) lets you **Rename** it or **Delete list**. Deleting asks first and removes the list's items and suggestions
for everybody.

## Add items

Type an item into **Add item** at the top of the list and tap Done or "+".

- Several items at once: separate them with commas or new lines: "milk, bread, eggs".
- Put the quantity at the end: "Milk 2 l", "Eggs 10", "Bread x2". It shows on the right of the name. A comma inside a
  number does not split items: "Milk 2.5%" is one item.
- If the item is already in the list, no second one appears: the app says "Already in the list", and a new quantity
  replaces the old one.
- While you type, matching names from this and the family's other lists appear under the field.

## Departments

Items are sorted into departments in the order you walk through a store: fruit and vegetables, bread, dairy and eggs,
meat and fish, and so on. Anything unknown goes to "Other".

If an item landed in the wrong place, touch and hold it, choose **Department** and the right one. The family's choice
is remembered: next time this item goes there by itself, in every list.

For lists that need no departments (for example "Gifts"), turn off **Group by department** in the list menu.

## In the store

- Tap an item to mark it bought. Bought items move down to the collapsed "Bought · 3" section. To put an item back,
  expand the section and tap it.
- No network is needed. Without one, the top of the list says "No network. Your ticks are saved and will be sent when
  the connection is back." When the connection is back, the ticks reach the others.
- If two of you shop from one list, you see each other's ticks within seconds.
- **Who is shopping**: if someone else ticked an item in a list during the last 15 minutes, the list card and Today
  show "Dmitry is shopping now". This comes only from the ticks; no location is used.

## After the trip

Bought items stay in the list until somebody removes them. In the list menu tap **Clear bought** and the list is
clean again. You can tap Undo right after.

Removed items are not lost: they become **suggestions**. Under the empty field you see buttons such as "+ Milk",
"+ Bread", "+ Eggs", the ones you buy most often first. One tap brings the item back with its department and note. To
stop suggesting an item, touch and hold the suggestion and confirm **Stop suggesting**.

## Edit an item

Touch and hold an item. Its sheet opens: name, quantity, note ("lactose free"), department, tags, who added it and who
bought it. Tap **Save**. **Remove from the list** removes the item but keeps it as a suggestion; you can tap Undo right
after.

## Tags

Tags can go on lists (list menu › **List tags**) and on single items (in the item sheet). When items in a list have
tags, buttons "All" and one per tag appear above the items, so you can keep, for example, only what is needed for the
cottage. Items added while a tag is selected get that tag.

## Who can do what

Everybody with access to Shopping sees all lists of the family. The rest is set by the administrator with groups
(More › Family). By default:

| | Adults | Kids |
| --- | --- | --- |
| See lists, who added and who bought | yes | yes |
| Add, edit, tick and remove items, change the department, remove suggestions | yes | yes |
| Create, rename and delete lists, tag a list, turn departments on or off | yes | no |

Members without access to Shopping see neither the Shopping tab nor the Today card and receive no list data. There are
no shopping notifications.
