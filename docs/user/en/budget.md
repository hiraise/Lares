# Budget

How each month's income is distributed into categories: the distribution plan, what actually came in, which
categories have been transferred, the shortfall and the free money. Lares does not track spending, accounts or
balances — only how income is distributed. Amounts are whole roubles.

## Where to find the budget

- **More › Budget** opens the current month. The ‹ › arrows move between months; tapping the month's name opens
  **All months**.
- **Today** has a **Budget** card: the next income not recorded yet ("December 20 · 160,000 ₽") and how many categories
  of the money that came in are not transferred yet ("Not transferred: 4 categories"). When everything has come in and
  is transferred, it says "All distributed". A tap opens the budget.
- Until there is a plan, the budget offers **Create plan**.

## The distribution plan

The plan is a table like a notebook: rows are categories, columns are incomes by day of the month. **Plan** in the
budget's top bar opens it with the tabs **Categories | Incomes | Table**.

- **Incomes**: a day of the month (1–31; the last day in shorter months) and the expected amount. Two incomes may share
  a day.
- **Categories**: a name, the monthly amount and how much of each income goes to the category. The sheet shows
  "Distributed 58,000 of 60,000".
- The summary on top, **Income 346,000 · Categories 346,000**, has a check mark when everything adds up. A row or
  column that does not add up is marked, and the summary says "off by N". You can save anyway.
- **Don't use** keeps a category out of new months but in the history. **Delete** is offered only for a category that
  no month has used.
- Reorder categories from the row menu: **Move up** / **Move down**.
- **Table** shows the whole plan like the notebook; sums that do not add up are highlighted.

Changes are a draft until you tap **Save**. The app then asks **from which month** they apply: the next month
(default) or the current one. Past months keep their plan. Saving from the current month replaces that month's
one-off changes with the plan; recorded amounts and ticks stay.

**Plan history** (the clock icon) lists all versions: from which month, who saved it and when. A tap shows the
version.

## A month

Each month opens from the plan in force for it.

- The big card on top: **Free 12,000 ₽** (more came in than was transferred), **Short 12,000 ₽** (less came in
  than expected) or **Expected 346,000 ₽** before anything is recorded. Below it: "Came in 150,000 of 346,000 ·
  transferred 138,000".
- **Incomes**: each with its date and amount, "Expected" or "Came in 150,000 · Dec 4", "Transferred 7 of 12".
- **Categories**: transferred of the monthly amount.

### Recording an income

Tap an income. **Came in** and **Date** start with the expected amount and today on the first tap — correct them if
something else came in. If less came in, the page shows the shortfall. **Clear** removes the record.

### Ticking transfers

The income's page lists its categories. When you have transferred the money in your bank, tick the category: the
planned amount is recorded. Transferred another amount? Tap the amount and enter it; the row then shows "20,000 of
27,000". **Tick all** ticks the rest. At the bottom you see how much of the money is still **Not distributed**.

## Extra income

A bonus or other income outside the plan: **Add income** under the incomes. The amount is required, a name is
optional ("Bonus"), the date is today unless you pick another. Extra incomes come after the planned ones, are not split
into categories and count fully as free money. A tap opens its page, where you can change the name, amount and date or
**Delete** it.

## Changing only this month

When this month should be different but the plan should not (for example, 30,000 for gifts in December instead of
10,000):

1. In the budget, open the **⋮** menu › **Change this month**.
2. The same editor opens, titled **December only**: change amounts, add a one-off category or switch one off (Don't
   use).
3. **Save** — only this month changes.

Everything that differs from the plan is marked with an orange dot: on categories and incomes, in the table and in the
month itself. A line **This month differs from the plan** appears under the big card; tapping it opens the month editor
again. The **⋮** menu › **Reset to the plan** removes the one-off changes; recorded amounts and ticks stay.

## Past months

Months are never deleted. **All months** shows each one: expected, came in and the result — free money or shortfall. A
tap opens the month.

## Who may do what

The family administrator decides this with groups (More › Family). By default:

| | Administrators | Adults | Kids |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| See the budget, the plan, every month and the history | yes | yes | no |
| Change the plan and a month, record incomes, tick transfers | yes | yes | no |

Members allowed only to look see the same without checkboxes and buttons. Members without access have no Budget
section and no Today card; budget data never reaches their phones. There is no per-category access.
