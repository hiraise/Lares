# Tasks

Shared to-dos and chores of the family: who does what and when. Everything works without internet; changes reach
the other phones as soon as there is a connection.

## Where to find your tasks

- **Today** (the first tab) shows your overdue tasks and today's tasks, and what you have already done today. Tick a
  task off right there. **All** opens the Tasks tab.
- **Tasks** (the second tab) lists the tasks by sections: Overdue, Today, Upcoming, No due date.
  - **Mine** shows tasks assigned to you and "anyone" tasks.
  - **All** shows the tasks of the whole family.
  - **Assigned by me** shows tasks you created for others.
  - **Tag** limits the list to one tag. The search and filter buttons are at the top.
- A **tag page** lists all open tasks with that tag. Open it from More › Tags, or tap a tag on a task's page.

Ticking a task off shows **Done · Undo** for a few seconds. Later you can open the task and tap **Mark as not done**.

## Create a task

Tap **Add** on Today or **Task** on the Tasks tab. Only the title is required:

- **Date** and **Time**: a task without a date sits under "No due date". A task without a time is due by the end of
  its day.
- **Repeat**: makes the task a routine (see below).
- **Assignees**: one or more family members, or **Anyone** ("whoever does it first"). A new task from Today or from
  "Mine" is assigned to you.
- **Tags**: for example "Kids" or "Car". You can create a new tag right here.
- **Reminder**: at the due time or some minutes, hours or days before. A task without a time reminds at 9:00. If
  notifications for Lares are off on the phone, the app asks you to allow them.
- **Tell me when it's done**: you get a notification when someone else completes your task.
- **Checklist**: steps inside the task, such as vacuum, dust, mop.

If several people are assigned, one completion is enough: the task is done for everybody.

## Routines (recurring tasks)

A routine shows only its **current** occurrence. When you complete or skip it, the next one appears. Missed
occurrences do not pile up: there is at most one overdue occurrence, and it is dropped when the next date comes.

There are two kinds:

- **On a schedule**: every day, on chosen weekdays, every month on the same day, every year. Example: "Take out the
  trash" every Monday and Thursday at 20:00. If nobody does it on Monday, it is overdue until Thursday, then Thursday's
  occurrence takes over.
- **After completion**: the next date counts from the day it was actually done. Example: "Replace the water filter"
  3 months after completion. Done two weeks late? The next one is due 3 months after that day.

A routine's page shows its history: who did it and when. To skip one occurrence, tap **Skip**. To delete a routine,
choose **Only this occurrence** or **The whole series**. To stop a routine, delete the series.

The checklist of a routine starts unticked for every occurrence.

## Reminders and notifications

- **Reminders** are set up on your phone and fire on time even without internet. They go to the assignees; for
  "anyone" tasks they go to everybody who sees tasks.
- **Notifications** come from the family server:
  - "Task assigned to me";
  - "Task completed", if you asked to be told;
  - "Task overdue", sent to the task's creator. For a task without a time it comes at 9:00 the next day.
- Instant notifications need the **ntfy** app on the phone; see [Instant notifications](instant-notifications.md).
  Without ntfy the app checks for news about every 15 minutes.
- Tap a notification or a reminder to open its task. If the task has been deleted, Lares opens the task list and says
  so.
- Turn off any kind in **More › Settings › Notifications › What to notify about**: Task assigned to me, Task
  completed, Task overdue, Task reminders.

## Who can do what

The family administrator decides this with groups (More › Family). By default:

| | Adults | Kids |
| --- | --- | --- |
| See all family tasks | yes | yes |
| Create tasks | yes | yes |
| Tick off their own tasks and "anyone" tasks | yes | yes |
| Change or delete other people's tasks | yes | no |

The creator of a task can always change it and tick it off. A member without access to tasks does not see the Tasks
tab and does not get task data.
